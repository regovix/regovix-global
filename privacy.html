Privacy Policy

How Regovix collects, uses, and protects your personal information across our global platform.

Last updated: September 2026

This page is a working draft, not a finalised legal document. It describes our actual current data-handling practices honestly, but privacy law varies significantly by jurisdiction (the EU's GDPR, California's CCPA, Australia's Privacy Act, and others each impose different obligations). Before relying on this policy for a specific market, it should be reviewed by a qualified privacy lawyer for that jurisdiction. If you're in Australia specifically, our Australian-law-specific policy at regovix.com.au/privacy is the version that governs the Australian entity.
Where your data goes — quick summary

Your input data (AI features) → Anthropic (USA) for processing → response returned to you
Your website traffic → Netlify (USA) hosting infrastructure
Your account and contact details → stored by Regovix

No data is sold to third parties. No data is used for advertising.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Regovix global platform at regovix.com, and to the applications it links to: IncidentLoop, FleetCheck, InductGuard, AuditMate, ChemVault, CompliBase, BarrierWatch, and EvidenceIQ. Each application requires its own account and sign-in.

2. What personal information we collect

Account and contact information

  • Name and job title
  • Business email address
  • Company name and business address
  • Username and encrypted password

Workplace compliance data

Depending on which Regovix applications your organisation uses, we may process incident reports, audit findings, chemical register entries, fleet inspection records, licence and induction records, and bow-tie/ICAM investigation records — including any personal information your organisation chooses to enter into those records (such as a worker's name against a training record).

Technical and usage data

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and features used
  • Login timestamps and session data

3. How we use your information

  • Providing and operating the Regovix applications
  • Creating and managing your account
  • Generating reports and evidence records on your behalf
  • Sending service-related notifications
  • Providing support
  • Improving the platform based on aggregated usage patterns

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

We may also disclose personal information if required by law, or in connection with a genuine business transfer such as a merger or acquisition — in which case affected customers will be notified.

5. International data transfers

Because our infrastructure providers are based in the United States, using Regovix means your data may be processed there. If you are located somewhere with specific rules about international data transfers (such as the EU under GDPR), please contact us before using the platform for data subject to those rules, so we can confirm an appropriate safeguard is in place.

6. Data security

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Access to customer data is restricted on a need-to-know basis
  • Role-based access controls within each application

7. How long we keep your information

Active account data is retained for the duration of your subscription or access period plus 30 days. Compliance records may need to be retained longer where your own regulatory obligations require it — that retention period is your organisation's responsibility to configure and manage.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, or to ask which specific rights apply to you, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to registered users. The current version is always available at regovix.com/privacy.
