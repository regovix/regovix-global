The part we're proudest of isn't a dashboard.

A corrective action that never rises above "retrain the person" is one of the most common ICAM weaknesses in real regulatory inspection findings — the organisational cause goes unaddressed, and the failure recurs. EvidenceIQ checks for this automatically, on every corrective action, regardless of who — or what — drafted it.

EVIDENCEIQ FLAGS Every action here stays at the individual level. No engineering control, procedure change, or resourcing fix addresses why the barrier was missing in the first place.

Checked the same way, every time

A person and an AI drafting a response get no benefit of the doubt from each other. The check runs identically either way — before the investigation gets filed and the pattern quietly repeats.

It's a narrow, specific check, not a general-purpose "AI safety score." We think narrow and specific is what makes it trustworthy.
